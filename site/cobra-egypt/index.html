One Egyptologist

A licensed Egyptologist, with a degree and a national licence, stays with you from the first morning to the last. Not a new face in every city.

Private access

Giza before the gates open, the Grand Egyptian Museum after the day visitors leave, tombs visited by official permit. Arranged where available, never promised blindly.

Dahabiya, not the big ship

Sail from Esna to Aswan on a traditional boat of six to twelve cabins. It moors where the 300-passenger ships cannot, and keeps no docking queue.

The journey, north to south

The shape most first journeys take: ten days, three cities, four nights on the river.

  1. Cairo & Giza3 nights

    The pyramids at opening, Saqqara and Dahshur in the order they were built, the Grand Egyptian Museum, and an evening in the old city.

  2. Fly southabout 1 hour

    Morning flight to Luxor.

  3. Luxor1–2 nights

    The West Bank from 6am: Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut, Medinet Habu. Karnak when the light is low.

  4. Board at Esna≈ 250 km of river

    Four or five nights by dahabiya, sailing south.

  5. El Kab · Edfu · Gebel el-Silsila · Kom Omboon the river

    Quarries, tombs and temples, several of them stops the large ships are not permitted to make.

  6. Aswan1–2 nights

    Philae at opening, the Nubian bank, a felucca at sunset, a historic hotel above the cataract.

  7. Further south280 km · 45 min by air

    Optional.

  8. Abu Simbela morning, or a night

    Ramses II's temples on Lake Nasser, best before 9am or at dusk after the day visitors fly out.

How we work

From your first message to your flight home, one plan and one person to call.

  1. Tell us your dates

    Who is travelling, roughly when, and what the journey is for. A few lines is enough.

  2. A plan within 48 hours

    A complete private plan in your inbox: hotels, boat, your Egyptologist, private access where available. No obligation.

  3. We hold what is scarce

    The guide and the dahabiya cabin first, then flights and hotels around them.

  4. One contact, all the way

    One person on WhatsApp, day and night, from landing in Cairo to leaving Aswan.

We plan and manage your journey ourselves. On the ground, it is delivered by licensed Egyptian tour operators, boat owners and guides we select and check. We own no buses and no boats, so we choose the right one for each part of your trip.
