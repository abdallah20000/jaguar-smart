Our story

A personal initiative, born from love.

Bella Charity Foundation was started by Dr. Belkais with one simple idea: go to the children, sit with them, and give them a day full of play, laughter and gifts.

Their purity and kindness are what bring us joy. Every visit reminds us that the smallest gesture can change a whole day — and sometimes a whole life.

🤍 We are a personal initiative and do not accept donations.
